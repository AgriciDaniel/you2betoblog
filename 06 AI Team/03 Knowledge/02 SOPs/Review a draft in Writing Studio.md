# Review a draft in Writing Studio

1. Enable Writing Studio and the youtubetoblog Home plugin, then reload Obsidian. Home provisions and selects the `YouTube to Blog` project at `03 Blogs`. Do not create a second project manually.
2. Open the binder. Posts sort by `binder-order` (date plus sequence), show a pipeline-owned `binder-status` stripe and show word count against `word-count-goal`.
3. Open the post and read it in Reading view. Check the hero, the frame captions with timestamps, the layout groups and the video figure.
4. The pipeline sets Draft, In Progress or Complete. Set Published only after you have checked the live post.
5. Leave `review.md` alone: the delivery gate checks it. Put your notes in the chat or apply them with the Alembic workflows ([[Polish with Alembic workflows]]).
6. After edits, ask the agent to re-run delivery for the blog; the evaluation in [[Home]] updates.
7. Use `Back to YouTube to blog` at the top of the Launcher or Binder to return to the main sidebar.
